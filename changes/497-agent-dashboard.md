### NEW

- The Discount code holder's students page is now a dashboard. It greets them by name ("Habari,
  Amina!") with their code, counts their students at each step, and lists who could use a
  reminder: 14 days after applying with no visit, 14 days after a visit with no interview, or 7
  days after passing the interview without enrolling. "Andaa ujumbe" opens a ready-made reminder
  to send from their own WhatsApp, or to copy. The page never shows a family's number.
- The student list can show one step at a time, as well as searching by name.
- Three ready-made share messages (to a parent, to a group, short) sit on the page while there
  are no students yet, and behind "Shiriki code yako" after that. Each names what the code
  takes off the interview fee now, such as TZS 10,000. The finish screen's WhatsApp message
  names it too, once the code takes something off.
- Right after setting their password, a Discount code holder sees "Umeidhinishwa!" once, with
  "Ona Wanafunzi Niliowaleta" on to the dashboard.
