### NEW

- The Admission form asks for names in boxes: the student's own name, then the father's first, middle and last names. Space moves to the next box, a pasted name spreads across them, and the student's full name shows as it is typed. Every box is needed, and names are saved in capitals.
- The custodian question has the switch "The father is the custodian". Turned on, it sets the relationship to Father, skips that question and goes to the phone. Turned off, the custodian's first, middle and last names are asked.
- Start over sits on the first question and asks before clearing a form with answers. On an empty form it says there is nothing to clear. On the finished screen it opens a new form at once.

### IMPROVED

- The Discount code box adds the hyphen by itself: AAN132 becomes AAN-132. A Promo code such as EID2026 still works when the box shows it as EID-2026.
- The Admission form's focus outline is easier to see: every button, choice and link now meets the 3:1 contrast a focus outline needs.
