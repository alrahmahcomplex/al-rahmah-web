### NEW

- Staff can check in a family that sent the Admission form. Type one of the children's Admission Numbers in Home's search bar, or press "Check in this family" on the Applicants side sheet or "Check in" on an "Expected at the desk" row. What the form gave is already filled in. Staff answer only the rest: the custodian of each child, Boy or girl where the form had no answer, the previous school from STD 2, whether the children are siblings, and who brought them. One save records the visit for all of them.
- A cousin or other relative's child on the same form gets their own custodian, so only real siblings get the 10% sibling discount.
