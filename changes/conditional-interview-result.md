### NEW

- An interview can be recorded as Conditional, next to Passed and Failed: the student is below the pass mark, and the school takes them on an agreement the family signs with the head of school. A Conditional student pays the School fee and enrols the way a Passed one does.
- The heads of school mark the agreement signed from the student's Next step card, with a new permission, "Mark agreement signed", given to their role on the Roles screen. Until it is marked, other staff see "Waiting for the agreement", and the Accountant's payment form warns that it isn't marked yet. The warning never stops the payment. The record shows when the agreement was signed and who marked it, and the history says "marked the agreement signed".
- A Conditional result has its own Swahili WhatsApp and SMS messages. They send the family to the Head of Primary School (Day Care to STD 7) or the Head of Secondary School (FORM 1 to FORM 4) to sign the agreement, then to pay the fee, and give that school's own number.
- The Interviews list filters by Conditional, and Reports counts Conditional interviews.
- Marketing agents see a Conditional result as "Amepokelewa kwa masharti".

### IMPROVED

- The Failed result messages give the school's own number (+255 677 330 099 for primary, +255 677 990 033 for secondary) instead of the admissions office number.
- Results show as coloured tags on the Interviews list and the student's record: Passed green, Conditional orange, Failed red.
