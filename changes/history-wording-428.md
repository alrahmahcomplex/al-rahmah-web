### FIXED

- An applicant's History now says in plain words what happened to their sibling group, Sibling discount, Manual Sibling fee edits, Sibling requests, seat in a stream and requests for a new stream, instead of showing database names such as "added a sibling_group_members record". Each entry names the sibling by name and Admission Number and the stream by name, and says why it happened. Ids, times and other fields staff don't need are left out.
- A Sibling discount entry no longer reads "Interview result", and a sibling group, Sibling discount or seat entry no longer reads "Closed with the application".
- Boy or girl now shows under that name in the History.
