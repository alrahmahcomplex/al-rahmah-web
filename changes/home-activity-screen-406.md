### NEW

- Home has an Activity log under the charts and numbers: everything that happened today and what
  still waits for someone, ten rows a page. Each row shows its time, what happened, the details
  and the next step:
  - families expected at the desk;
  - new applications and re-applications;
  - interview results ready to send;
  - discount requests and claimed Discount codes;
  - school fees paid, full classes and call backs.
- Pressing a row opens its details at the side.
- Each person sees the rows and buttons their role allows. Only the Admissions Manager approves
  discounts and confirms claimed codes; an Officer sees the requests without those buttons, and no
  claimed codes.
