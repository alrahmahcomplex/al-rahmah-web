### IMPROVED

- Home's charts are cleaner. The tabs run across the top of the card, and the Admission year list sits at the bottom right with no words beside it. The sentence over each chart and "See the numbers" are gone, since each chart shows its own numbers.
- Months is a curved line across the whole calendar year, January to December. Each month counts that month's applications whatever year they came in, so a family that enrolled late still shows. The month still running is lighter, with a dashed line into it.
- Classes are bars, one per class, each named in full, with no Nursery, STD and FORM row under them.
- Status shows a bar per status, from Applied at the top to Enrolled and Declined, each with its number.
- Seats shows who holds each class's seats: paid in full at the bottom, then partial payment (a Deposit or First instalment), flat on top. A key under the chart names the shades.
- The Sources ring's slices end square with small round corners, so a small source shows close to its real size.
- On a phone the labels under the bars, and "N free" or "Full" over the Seats capsules, stand upright so every name shows in full.
