### NEW

- Home has its chart card, with four tabs:
  - **Classes:** students per class as a smooth line, with the classes grouped Nursery, STD and FORM.
  - **Months:** applications per month this year as columns, with the current month lighter.
  - **Seats:** each class as a capsule showing "N free" or a red "Full".
  - **Sources:** a ring with the year's total in the middle, split into the Admission form, Agents' Discount codes, Promo codes and Walk-in at the desk.

  Each tab opens with a sentence the numbers say, such as "September was the busiest month, with 21 applications". Hovering or tabbing to a mark shows its number, and "See the numbers" shows them as a table. Staff see only the tabs whose numbers their role already sees: the applications need permission to view applicants, and the seats need permission to view payments. A tab with nothing to show yet is left out.
