### NEW

- Interviews has a search box that narrows the list as you type, by any part of a student's name or by the Admission Number, across the whole enrollment year. The letters that matched are marked in light orange.
- Clicking a student on Interviews opens their interview in a side sheet, and the list stays where it was. Staff who record interviews can record or correct the result there, and the Accountant can mark the fee paid. View full record opens the student's record on the Interview tab, and its title, "Interviews › name", leads back to the same list.
- Each column on Interviews sorts with the small arrows after its name: lowest first, then highest first, then back to newest first. Registered, Class, Result, Interview fee and Result sent filter from their headings too. Several classes can be picked at once.

### IMPROVED

- Interviews opens with the newest registration at the top.
- The four rows of filter pills are gone. The year is a dropdown, a date choice shows one day's registrations, and the Filter button holds Class, Result, Interview fee and Result sent (now Sent or Not sent).
- The table shows the day each student registered after the S/N, and no longer shows Day or boarding (the side sheet still does). The total sits beside the Interviews title, and under the table it reads "Showing 1–50 of 142 interviews" before the page numbers.
