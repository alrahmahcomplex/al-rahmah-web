### IMPROVED

- The language card on a first visit to the Admission form and the Discount code pages has no timer. It no longer picks Kiswahili after 5 seconds; it waits until the parent or agent taps a language. Tapping outside the card or pressing Escape leaves it open.
- After the tap, the card folds straight back into the Menu button. The 3-second confirmation, its filling bar and the small phone animation are gone.
