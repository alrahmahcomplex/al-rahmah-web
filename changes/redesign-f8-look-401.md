### IMPROVED

- The redesigned staff screens take the look of prototype v38: lines half as thick, a fainter lavender background on every page, two-colour icons, and soft line pictures in place of the isometric ones when a list is empty or something is loading.
- Buttons that delete, decline or clear are now outlined in red, and buttons that step back are outlined in dark orange. Only the orange button that moves you forward is filled. No button is filled red anywhere, including "clear the form" in the Admission form's menu.
- Notes and pop-up messages have a faint tint and a thin line in their own colour. A pop-up message can now also say that something went wrong.
- A finished registration ends with the Admission form's green check mark, and confetti bursts out of it once the tick is drawn, on phones and computers alike.
