### NEW

- Today, Check-in and Register students are now one page, Home, the first entry in the staff sidebar. Type a family's Admission Number in the bar at the top to open their student's record; Register students sits beside it and, once you scroll past it, waits as a round orange plus at the edge of the page.
- Two numbers on Home: interviews held today and students who applied this week, each with how it compares with a week earlier and a small line of the last 7 days. Pick an earlier day or week from the round button on each card, and Reset brings back today and this week.
- Quick actions on Home: Call a family back, Seats and Request a discount, each for the staff who can open it.

### IMPROVED

- Old Check-in links open Home. The sidebar no longer has Today, Check-in or Register a student.
