### IMPROVED

- Applicants now has the new staff look and sits in the new staff shell. The list is a card with a pill for each status, and the closure and group invitation filters sit beside them. The table shows the student with their Admission Number, the class, the custodian, the status and, for staff who see payments, the Seat priority. Pages are numbered pills.
- Clicking a student, or pressing Enter on one, opens a side panel with the student's details, the custodian, the School fee and the next step. "Open full record" opens the full student page. Record payment opens the record's School fee tab.
- Searching is done from the top bar only. A search shows as "Search: …" above the list, with Clear, and Clear brings back the filters that were set before.
- Import and Export open the new sheets, with the same steps and choices as before. Register students sits in the page header.
