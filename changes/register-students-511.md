### NEW

- Register students and Check-in now let staff choose whether a student’s last name is the father’s last name or his middle name once both are typed.
- Previous school names are typed in capitals. Region and Country use type-ahead fields with lists that open downward: Outside Tanzania first, then A to Z, and Country with flags.
- A school Outside Tanzania needs its country when staff enter it or change its name or region, on Register students, Check-in, the Family page and the applicant record, which now has the country box too. For another school, the saved country shows first in the list and staff pick it again to confirm. Changing only private or public keeps the saved country.
- An older school Outside Tanzania saved without a country stays as it is. Check-in asks for the country, and Not known yet keeps the school unchanged.
- Custodian opens with two choices, The father or Someone else, showing only the fields that choice needs. From the second question on, Back sits on the left and Start over moves after the section chip, and phone keyboards keep the active box in view.
- Finished registrations and check-ins now offer Register for interview, one student at a time or Register all for interview, with a green Registered for interview chip once done. Beside it are Register another student (or Check in another family) and View full record (or Open this family’s full record for 2 or more students).
