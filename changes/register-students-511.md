### NEW

- Register students and Check-in now let staff choose whether a student’s last name is the father’s last name or his middle name once both are typed.
- Previous school names are typed in capitals, and Region and Country use type-ahead fields with downward overlay lists (Outside Tanzania first, then A to Z; Country with flags, required for a school Outside Tanzania). Check-in prompts for the country on older records saved as Outside Tanzania without one.
- Custodian opens with two choices, The father or Someone else, showing only the fields that choice needs. From the second question on, Back sits on the left and Start over moves after the section chip, and phone keyboards keep the active box in view.
- Finished registrations and check-ins now offer Register for interview (one student at a time or Register all for interview, with a green Registered for interview chip once done), alongside Register another student (or Check in another family) and View full record (or Open this family’s full record for 2+ students).
