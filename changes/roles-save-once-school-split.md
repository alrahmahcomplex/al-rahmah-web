### IMPROVED

- Staff and roles saves once. Select and clear a role's permissions and the roles it manages as much as you like; nothing is saved until you press Save changes. A bar at the bottom of the card counts the changes waiting, with Undo changes beside Save changes, and each changed box shows Added or Removed.
- Saving sends every change in one go. If anything fails, nothing is saved and your selections stay on screen to try again. Another Super Admin's changes to other boxes on the same role are kept.
- Leaving the page, or opening another role, with changes not saved asks first: save them, leave without saving, or keep editing.
- History shows one entry per save, listing each permission and managed role added or removed.
- A permission that needs another names it: "Needs “View school-fee payments”" beside Record campus visits, for example. Selecting it selects the one it needs too.
- The screens say "select" instead of "tick": the export's Choose columns, the Pre-Form One and sibling boxes on an applicant and their History lines, and, in English, the security check's prompt on sign-in, the Admission form and the Discount code forms.
