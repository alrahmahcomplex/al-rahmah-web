### NEW

- A Promo code's sheet has a **Share promo code** button, the one orange button while nothing is being edited. It opens the code's link for parents (the Admission form with the code filled in) and a ready message to send: for one parent, for a group, or short, in English or Kiswahili. Staff can change the message before sending it, copy it or the link, or press **Share on WhatsApp**. If the link is taken out of the message, the sheet says so and puts it back on request; **Reset message** brings back the suggested words.
- Every role that can open Promo codes can share an Active one, including roles that can't change codes. Paused, Expired and Ended codes have no Share button, and the sheet says why.
- The design kit has a new part, the **Message box**: a ready message the person can change, with a copy button in its corner. It is on the Design system page.

### IMPROVED

- On a Promo code's sheet, Save and Cancel show once a field changes. Cancel puts the saved values back, and saving keeps the sheet open on the code.
