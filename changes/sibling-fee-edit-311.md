### NEW

- Staff who record payments can edit a student's Sibling fee on the record and in the Applicants
  side sheet: Full fee, Sibling 10% or Back to automatic, with a reason and an optional note.
  Before saving, they see what each sibling would pay.
- A student granted the Sibling discount after enrolling shows "Granted: fee edit needed". Staff
  who record payments apply the 10%, or leave the fee as it is with a reason.
- While a Sibling request waits, the payment form says the fee is recorded in full for now and can
  be edited if the request is granted.
- Reports has a Sibling discount follow-up card that counts the cases needing a look: requests
  waiting, typing mistakes confirmed, previous schools to add, fees edited by hand and fee edits
  needed. A count opens the Applicants list of those students.
- The Applicants list has a Sibling discount column.

### IMPROVED

- The custodian's details on the record and in the side sheet say who brought the student.
