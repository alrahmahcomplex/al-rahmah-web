### NEW

- The application record and the Applicants side sheet show the student's sibling group. Each
  sibling has a fee tag (Sibling 10%, Full fee, Full fee for now) and one sentence saying why, in
  English or Kiswahili.
- Staff can correct the father's full name on the record. When the new name is close to a
  sibling's, they are asked "One name, or two?" before anything is saved. When it no longer
  matches, the record says which fees changed and offers the Sibling request.
- Staff can add or correct the previous school on the record, or mark it Not known yet.
- Staff can send a Sibling request from the record, also for an Enrolled student, with a note on
  the proof the family will bring.
- Discount requests lists Sibling requests too. Before granting one, the Admissions Manager sees
  how the group's fees would look.

### IMPROVED

- The application history names the father's full name, where it came from, who brought the
  student, the custodian's relationship and the previous school in plain words.
- Register students: the note for the Admissions Manager is required when the fathers' names
  don't match, as the database already requires.
