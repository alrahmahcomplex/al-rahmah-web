### NEW

- Seats are now counted in streams, such as FORM 1 A (Boys). The seats each class had for Day and for Boarding became one Mixed stream A with the two numbers added together, and the children who already held a seat were placed in it in ranking order. A class with no seats set keeps no limit.
- A child takes a seat by themselves: the first stream of their class, in order, that takes them and has a free seat. When no stream can take a child, their school fee can't be recorded until the Admissions Manager opens a stream. Staff can send that request from the child's School fee tab or from Seats.
- Seats shows each class's streams, the children waiting for a stream, and the requests. The Admissions Manager opens streams there, changes their label and seats, and brings last year's streams up for a new year.
- The student export's Full data has a "Boy or girl" column.

### IMPROVED

- The Fee schedule no longer has seat boxes. Day or boarding now only sets the fee.
- Reports and Home count seats by stream.
