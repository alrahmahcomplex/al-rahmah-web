### NEW

- Seats are now counted in streams, such as FORM 1 A (Boys). The seats each class had for Day and for Boarding became one Mixed stream A with the two numbers added together, and the children who already held a seat were placed in it in ranking order. A class with no seats set, or with 0 on one side and nothing set on the other, keeps no limit.
- A child takes a seat by themselves: the first stream of their class, in order, that takes them and has a free seat. A child who holds no seat yet can't have a school fee payment recorded while no stream can take them. The payment goes through once a seat frees or the Admissions Manager opens a stream; staff can ask for one from the child's School fee tab or from Seats. A child who already holds a seat can always go on paying.
- Seats shows each class's streams, the children waiting for a stream, and the requests. Everyone who can view payments finds it in the sidebar. The Admissions Manager opens streams there, changes their label and seats, and brings last year's streams up for a new year.
- The student export's Full data has a "Boy or girl" column.

### IMPROVED

- The Fee schedule no longer has seat boxes. Day or boarding now only sets the fee.
- Reports and Home count seats by stream.
