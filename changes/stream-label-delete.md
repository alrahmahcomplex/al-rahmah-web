### NEW

- A class's first stream no longer needs a label, and shows as the class alone, for example "STD 1". Every stream added next to it needs a label, and the suggestion starts at B. A boys stream and a girls stream may both go without one ("FORM 1 (Boys)", "FORM 1 (Girls)").
- The Admissions Manager can delete a stream nobody is placed in, with the pencil on Seats. It asks once more, then the stream goes for good and the streams after it move up one place. A child's history still names it, marked "(deleted)".
- "Add a stream" now has the Label and Seats names inside their boxes, like the pencil sheet.

### IMPROVED

- A class whose only stream was labelled A loses the A, so it shows as "STD 1" rather than "STD 1 A".
