### NEW

- The Admissions Manager can change who a stream takes (Boys, Girls or Mixed) with the pencil on Seats. Before saving, the sheet shows which children have to move and the stream each one goes to. If no stream has room, the same save can open a new stream or add seats to another one. The Manager can also let the children wait, and the sheet says how many will have no seat. Children the stream no longer takes go to the first stream of their class that takes them and has a seat, after the children already waiting. Their history says "Who the stream takes was changed".
- Each stream on Seats shows how many boys and girls are placed in it.
- The design kit has a named field: the field's icon and name stay inside the box when it holds a value. The Change sheet uses it for Label and Seats.
